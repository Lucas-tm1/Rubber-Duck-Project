Dr. Chase

Also known as Mr. Webber this website has a lot on him.

Age: 23 years old

Favorite Color: Blue Favorite Food: Thick Crust Pepperoni Pizza Favorite Hobby: Helping those in need Personality: Very smart, hardworking, very stern. Occupation/Dream Job: Doctor Favorite Song or Music: Bracelet by Lauv Favorite Movie/Game/TV Show: Valorant

My duck is named Dr. Chase however by many he is known as Mr. Webber. Chase tends to be a serious duck always focused on the job in front of him. With his dedication Chase became one of the best doctors in the world also getting the position at quite a young age for a doctor. Along with this work he puts his best efforts into helping others anytime he can. With this hardworking attitude he tends to get a lot of praise however many dislike how serious he could be all the time. Though he has proved to earn their respect with how good he got.
